# Same Cord

**Format name:** Same Cord
**Series:** Same Cord #1
**Logline:** The word is one cord down a vertical frame. Each fixed phrase is a clip on that cord, and you learn the word by watching who is left holding it.
**Episode:** agent, inside free agent, double agent, agent provocateur, change agent, and agent of chaos.
**Runtime:** 60.0 seconds, 9:16 (1080×1920), 30 fps, 1800 frames. Frame 1800 matches frame 0.

No title card and no bug. The first frame is already the hook, so a replay can cut seamlessly.

## Visual system and animation style

Flat paper-cut, hard offset shadows, no faces, no lipsync, no gradients except a full-frame color wash. One cotton cord (#F3EDE3) hangs from the top edge to a cream badge at optical center (540, 860). The holder, when there is one, is only a cropped hand entering from y=0. The hand never becomes a person.

The only kinetic type is **AGENT**: condensed grotesque, ~180 px, letter-drop on impact, color-split when the cord splits, a short shake when the cord frays. Every other on-screen word is static, 2–4 words, sitting on the badge next to the cord. Nothing below y=1500 (platform UI). Nothing important above y=180 except the cropped hand. Bottom captions, when used, sit at y≈1420, beside the badge, not as a transcript bar.

Palette is the pattern interrupt. Indigo #14122B (hook and loop), brass #3C2E12 (false boss), indigo plus a top rim light (the real hand), sky #7EC8E3 (free agent), a hard vertical split #1F4E79 / #8E1E2F (double agent), theatre black #0E0E10 (agent provocateur), grey #8A8A8A (the pause), office green #1E3A2F (change agent), ember #2A1210 (agent of chaos), then indigo again.

Motion is spring-based: a tug, a snap, a fray. One soft tick marks each state change. Music is a low plucked bed, ducked under the voice. The voice is warm and close, and it slows on every spoken "agent." The frayed cord (the surprise) holds an extra second after the line ends so it can be encoded.

Sound-off read of the cord: empty, crowned, held, open, doubled, masked, frozen, held by a company, frayed, empty again.

## Chunks

Five chunks, one word. The false boss at 2.0–6.0 is the wrong belief the hook knocks down, not a sixth chunk.

1. **8.0–12.0** — these phrases keep the agent and hide who they act for.
2. **12.0–18.0** — free agent: the team let go.
3. **18.0–24.0** — double agent: two services, one being played.
4. **24.0–30.0** — agent provocateur: the crowd is a costume.
5. **33.0–51.2** — change agent is still held; agent of chaos names the mess.

## Series plan

Named, numbered series: **Same Cord**. Later episodes start at 0.0–3.0 with a spoken recall card, "Same Cord recall," then the new word. The quiz is the cold open, not a joke inside the episode. One cord, new clips.

1. **Same Cord #1 — agent.** This episode.
2. **Same Cord #2 — break.** One stick takes the clips break the ice, break a leg, and break even; the cold open asks whether a free agent's cord is open or held.
3. **Same Cord #3 — cold.** Cold shoulder, cold feet, and cold turkey; the cold open asks who a free agent acts for, and whether break a leg snaps the stick.
4. **Same Cord #4 — hand.** Out of hand, heavy hand, and in good hands; the cold open asks whether break even snaps or balances, and whether cold turkey is a temperature.
5. **Same Cord #5 — call.** Close call, call off, and on call; the cold open asks whether anyone holds the cord in agent of chaos, and whose hand "in good hands" names.

## Production

**Remotion, difficulty medium.** One React composition: cord, badge, cropped hand, kinetic AGENT, and the color washes, timed with `useCurrentFrame` and springs. Frame 1800 is specified as a copy of frame 0, so the loop is a render property, not an edit hope. Three.js would build a flat cord in 3D for no teaching gain. Manim's camera and type fights a vertical social cut. Medium, not high: no rigs, no filmed plates, no simulation. Medium, not low: eight cord states have to read with the sound off, the voice has to hit the same frame as the clip, the 3-second hold cannot be cut short in the edit, and the last frame has to match the first.

## What this format refuses to be

Same Cord refuses to be a meaning tree, a timeline, a hotspot scene, a word duel, a doer-and-done-to swap, a myth-then-fact card, a fill-in-the-blank sentence, or a terminal.

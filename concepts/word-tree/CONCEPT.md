# WORD TREE

**Episode:** Word Tree #1  
**Word:** agent  
**Frame:** 1080×1920 (9:16)  
**Duration:** 48.0 s

## Logline

A bleach bottle and a spy share one root. Viewers already use *agent* for a person with keys, a spy, and a liquid on a label, but they have not said the rule those uses share: the agent is the one doing it. The film grows that rule as a tree and withholds it until the last spoken line.

## Storyboard

Durations: 2.0 + 6.0 + 7.5 + 7.5 + 6.8 + 3.0 + 3.4 + 3.2 + 2.8 + 3.2 + 2.6 = **48.0 s**.

The root is the word. Each meaning is a branch. Each branch blooms once, into a collocation. Three chunks, nothing beside them: bleaching agent, secret agent (bloom: double agent), free agent. The keys person is the crowded picture from the opening, not a fourth lesson. No software sense, no screen, no terminal.

Major color washes change the whole frame. Inside each beat the picture also changes about every 2 seconds.

## Visual system and animation

Vertical composition, safe zones for a phone UI:

- Right keep-out x = 940–1080 (like column).
- Bottom keep-out y = 1640–1920 (platform captions and handle).
- Top keep-out y = 0–140.
- Caption pill: one line, y = 160–250, inside x = 80–900. Large enough to read on a phone.
- Canopy, branches, flowers: y = 280–1100, nothing past x = 900.
- Root: the word AGENT is the root mass, sitting on a soil line at y = 1560, letter height about 160 px, optically centered in the safe width. A thumb on the platform UI does not cover the cap height.

Style is flat ink on paper. Branches are one growing SVG stroke (dash offset), about 400 ms per shoot. Flowers pop with a short overshoot and then sit still. Icons are flat: bottle, cloth, eye behind leaves, price tag, snapped line, keys. No 3D, no camera move beyond a 4% scale breathe, no characters acting a scene, no pins, no hotspot labels.

**Kinetic type is only the word AGENT.** It may squash, crack, and flash. Flower labels and the caption pill use a regular grotesque, fade in, and never travel letter by letter. The kinetic face is a heavy condensed sans used for that one word only.

Color is the pattern interrupt: rose (the crowded twig), amber (liquid), indigo (hidden), green (unbound), then paper again for the reveal. Each wash is a hard 8-frame fill of the whole background.

Sound: one warm human voice, conversational, slower on every spoken *agent*. A single plucked line under the voice, ducked, and fully silent from 29.80 to 32.80. One tick when a branch locks. One crack when the fat twig shrinks. Nothing else.

## Series plan

Named, numbered series: **Word Tree**. Platform title of this film: “Word Tree #1 — agent”. The number is not burned into the picture, so the loop frame stays clean.

Episode 1 is the script above.

2. **Word Tree #2 — charge.** A charge is a load placed on someone or something — a fee, an accusation, or a battery — and the cold open asks whether the free agent in #1 was unpaid.
3. **Word Tree #3 — current.** A current is something running — a river, a wire, or the present — and the cold open asks what job the agent was doing in #1 and which charge sits in a battery (#2).
4. **Word Tree #4 — concrete.** Concrete is the heavy stuff you can kick, which is why a concrete plan is a plan with weight, and the cold open asks which current you can see, the river or the present (#3).
5. **Word Tree #5 — state.** A state is how something stands — a condition, a country, or a fact you stand up in a sentence — and the cold open asks what the agent was in #1 and whether concrete was the plan or the feeling (#4).

Later episodes quiz one or two earlier words before the new tree grows. Agent returns at #2, #3, and #5.

## Production

**Remotion. Difficulty: medium.**

The picture is a flat SVG stroke, three flowers, type, and burned-in captions. Remotion holds the 48.0 s clock, the 3.0 s silence, and a last frame that is the first frame. Springs and `interpolate` cover the growth and the single kinetic word, so a second animation library is unnecessary. Three.js would add a camera and a light the picture does not use. Manim would read as a math lecture under a warm voice.

Medium, because the re-hook clock, the safe zones, and the loop seam have to land on the frame. Not high: no 3D, no acted characters, no simulation.

## What this format refuses to be

Word Tree refuses to be a definition card that prints a gloss and then reads the gloss aloud.

## Measurement

Judge the cut by the share still watching at 2 seconds, average percentage viewed and where the retention curve dips, plus saves per reach and sends per reach — not by raw views; a bump at the tail means the loop earned a replay.

# Passing Hand

**Logline:** A flight sits stuck on the far side of a glass service window. The sign on the glass never moves it. The hand that carries it through, for you, is the word.

**Episode:** Passing Hand 1 — agent
**Frame:** 9:16 (1080×1920), 48.0 seconds
**Worked example:** booking a flight. The mix-up this episode catches is agent / agency: people tap the office on the door and say the agency booked it. The agency is the room. The agent is the person who acts for you. One word is taught. Agency is named once, as the sign, and is not given its own chunks.

## Visual system and animation style

Vertical POV from your side of a service window. Glass occupies the top 70%. A vertical slot sits just right of center, from y≈420 to y≈1280. Your hand is cropped along the bottom 15%, so the phone is the counter. The only saturated object is the coral pass `#E07A5F`, until the hand takes a warm edge. The office behind the glass is flat grey shapes, never a place to tap for labels.

The hand is eight SVG poses, crossfaded, not a rig: P1 reach, P2 pinch, P3 draw through, P4 offer to the pocket, P5 carry down, P6 release, P7 deliver to you, P8 withdraw. Motion is snappy in, soft settle. The camera stays locked; state changes are cuts, scale punches (100→115 on the cuff), and color. The aha image — the locked word on the hand — holds from 33.4 to 36.0.

**Type.** One heavy condensed grotesk, animated (scale, tracking, settle), used only for AGENT. Captions are a small humanist sans, lower third, 2–4 words, cream on a dark chip or ink on cream, burned in and beat-timed. The door sign is environmental paint: static, smaller, never the kinetic face. No arrows, no hotspot pins, no title card.

**Sound.** Warm close voice. Music is a muted pulse ducked under the voice. One soft tick on each re-hook. Silence from 27.2 to 29.5. Slow down on every spoken “agent.”

**Sound off.** The captions plus the picture carry it: tap the sign, the sign never moves the pass, the cuff word, the pocket spits, the streak dies when the hand lets go, the two-chip pause, the sign falls, the word locks, the pass lands in your hand, the glass frosts again.

**Spoken encounters with “agent” in one pass:** hook (0.0), chunk 1 (7.3), chunk 2 (13.5), reveal (33.4), payoff (36.2). The line at 46.0 is the hook played again, not a new lesson. Whenever the word is spoken, the kinetic word is on screen beside the hand. It is absent during the pause.

## Series plan — Passing Hand

Named, numbered. The hand, the glass, and the question “what actually moves?” stay the format. From episode 2 on, the first seconds quiz earlier words by name and number, then the new pair starts. The callback is the lesson, not a bumper.

1. **Agent** (this episode). Sign or hand: who booked the flight?
2. **Affect / effect.** The hand reaches through and changes the date on the pass (affect); a date that is already changed, held up on the far side, is the effect. Opens with a two-second quiz: “Passing Hand 1 — sign or hand?”
3. **Borrow / lend.** The pass moving toward you is lend; the pass leaving your hand is borrow. Opens by quizzing agent (sign or hand) and affect (the reach that changes the date, or the date held up).
4. **Imply / infer.** The hand slides a note across the slot (imply); your hand closes on it (infer). Opens by quizzing borrow / lend and agent.
5. **Fewer / less.** The hand passes three separate passes (fewer); a poured heap of sand will not split into passes (less). Opens by quizzing imply / infer and affect.

## Production

**Remotion**, difficulty **medium**. The film is flat shapes, eight hand poses, frame-locked type, and burned-in captions — a React composition with `useCurrentFrame` and springs, not a 3D pane and not a math scene. Medium because the cuff word has to hit the spoken syllable, the 3.0s pause has to stay empty, and frame 48.0 has to equal frame 0.0. It is not high: no character acting, no simulation, no camera move.

## What this format refuses to be

A myth told and then corrected, a lobby dotted with hotspot callouts, a sentence with a blank, a doer-versus-done-to swap, or a stack of software agents.

## Measurement

Judge the cut on the share still watching at 2s, average percent viewed and the retention curve, engaged-view rate, and saves plus sends per reach — not raw views. A rewatch spike on the loop is the signal the seam works. Do not ask people to share.
